# Product contract

FORGE serves one person pursuing a configurable long-term goal. The example business objective uses a 5,000,000 KZT monthly target. Default priorities: Business 45%, Builder 30%, Health 20%, Network 5%. Career capability is a useful alternative, with a compact skill graph for Python, APIs, databases, AI systems, automation, product engineering and sales.

The daily loop is: open → understand the highest-value outcome → leave and do real work → return with evidence → receive meaningful progress. Three mandatory quests are the default, with optional work separate. AI previews proposals and requires acceptance; local rules control progress. Completed and manual work survive replanning. No course-consumption, duration, commit-count or activity-count reward shortcut is provided.

The Command Center shows rating, rank, level, priorities, income goal, weekly boss and a Ghost calculated from actual history. Business keeps qualified leads, conversations, demos, proposals, shipped work and received payments. Skills progress through prerequisites and require independent practical proof for verification/mastery. Switching a skill state earns no XP. Recovery is a single small achievable action with reduced losses, not a full-day reward.

Analytics cover today, 7/30/90 days and all history, with bounded chart rendering and a paged transaction ledger. Unknown Health/coding/screen readings are not treated as measured zero. Health components describe execution and are not medical assessments. Planned rest avoids Health loss.

Onboarding collects objective, target/baseline income, weights, schedule, training, distraction notes, reward preferences, strictness and coaching tone without requiring credentials. Permissions are requested only for explicit integration actions. Sparse reminders can be disabled. Settings is limited to useful rules, preferences and operations; no social feed, avatars, marketplace or public profiles.

Demo/test stores are separate. Start Over and Purge have different disclosed consequences. Save failures preserve the published state. Storage errors never silently create an empty workspace. See the acceptance and capability matrices for verified platform behavior and remaining account/device gates.
